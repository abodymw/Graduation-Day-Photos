Privacy Policy

This site was built for one purpose: collecting and sharing photos from a specific graduation day among friends. It is a small personal project, not a commercial product, and there are no user accounts or tracking of any kind.

What gets stored

Photos that anyone uploads through this site are stored in a Firebase Storage bucket and are visible and downloadable by anyone who has the site's link. That's the entire point of the site: making sure everyone can find and download every photo from the day.

Google Drive import

If you use the "Google Drive" button to import a photo, the site asks Google for permission to access only the specific file(s) you pick in the picker window (using Google's drive.file scope). The site cannot see, list, or access any other file in your Drive. The picked photo's bytes are downloaded once and uploaded to this site's Firebase Storage bucket, exactly as if you had picked it from your device instead.

What isn't collected

No emails, names, analytics, or advertising identifiers are collected by this site. No data is sold or shared with third parties beyond what's needed to display the photos back to guests with the link.
